FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q01.Is MBBS abroad a good option for Indian students?

Yes, MBBS abroad is highly recommended for students who could not get a government medical seat in India. Universities are NMC/WHO approved and degrees are globally recognized.

Q02.What is the total cost of MBBS abroad?

Total cost ranges from ₹ 18 Lacs (Uzbekistan) to ₹ 90 Lacs (Czech Republic). Popular options like Georgia cost ₹ 25-35 Lacs total including hostel and food.

Q03.Is NEET required for MBBS abroad?

Yes, NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students to be eligible for the NMC screening test (NExT/FMGE) after completing MBBS abroad.

Q04.Is there any donation or capitation fee?

No. In countries like Georgia, Uzbekistan, Russia, Kazakhstan and most European universities, there is NO donation. You pay only tuition and hostel fees.

Q05.How long does the admission process take?

Typically 30-60 days from application to visa. We handle the complete process including university application, documentation and visa.

Q06.Do you provide FMGE/NExT coaching?

Yes, we provide dedicated FMGE/NExT online coaching to our students so they can practice India alongside their MBBS abroad.
