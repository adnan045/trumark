IMAT Exam Guide

The Complete IMAT Guide: MBBS in Italy

The International Medical Admissions Test (IMAT) is the single, centralized entrance exam conducted by the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR) for admission into English-taught Medicine and Surgery (MBBS) and Dentistry programs at public Italian universities.

An end-to-end breakdown covers everything from the exam format to the reality of the selection process, your chances, and fallback paths.

1 The IMAT Exam Overview

The exam is a 100-minute, pen-and-paper test consisting of 60 multiple-choice questions (5 options each). The maximum possible score is 90 points.

2 Selection Criteria & Rankings

Italy uses a highly strict regulatory split between EU and Non-EU applicants.

The Candidate Split

  • EU Candidates: Includes EU citizens, Italian citizens, and non-EU citizens who legally hold a long-term residency permit in Italy.
  • Non-EU Candidates: Students applying from outside Europe (e.g., India, UK, USA). Crucial Rule: Non-EU candidates can apply to only one university choice.

Cut-Offs & "Scrolling"

Admission is purely merit-based, determined by your national ranking.

  • EU Ranking: Uses a system called "scrolling." EU applicants list multiple universities. If they don't get into their 1st choice, they can be "booked" for a lower choice and wait for weeks as higher-ranked students drop out, allowing the ranking list to "scroll" down.
  • Non-EU Ranking: Because you can only pick one university, you only compete against the non-EU students who picked that exact same school. There is a minimal system of scrolling, but if you miss your specific university's cut-off, you generally cannot switch to another university that has empty seats.

What are your realistic chances?

Your success heavily depends on your strategic choice of university and your ability to target safe scores.

  • EU Safe Scores: Historically hover around the low 50s because the scrolling mechanism allows students with lower scores to eventually secure a spot as choices shift.
  • Non-EU Safe Scores: Significantly more competitive. For top-tier universities like Milano-Bicocca or Bologna, cut-off scores routinely spike above 65+ points. For mid-tier or southern universities (like Bari, Luigi Vanvitelli, or Messina), cut-offs can fall between 50 to 56 points.

4 Timeline & Process Flow

The application process involves multiple mandatory steps before you even sit for the test.

  1. 1 Pre-Enrollment & DV/CIMEA: April – July. Non-EU students must apply for pre-enrollment via the Universitaly portal and request a Declaration of Value (DV) from the Italian Embassy or a CIMEA statement to prove high school eligibility.
  2. 2 Official IMAT Registration: Late August – Early September. Register on the Universitaly website, select your test center (e.g., New Delhi, London, Dubai, or Italian cities), pay the fee (~€130), and lock in your university choice.
  3. 3 The Exam: Mid-September. Take the 100-minute physical exam at 11:00 AM Italian time (adjusted locally for international test centers).
  4. 4 Rankings & Enrollment: October. Anonymous scores drop first, followed by the definitive national merit ranking. Admitted students must rapidly submit their visa and university enrollment files.

5 Future Opportunities

Graduating from an Italian public medical school offers massive advantages:

  • Globally Recognized Degree: You earn an MD (Doctor of Medicine) degree. It is fully compliant with EU directives, meaning you can practice anywhere in the European Union without rewriting foundational licensing exams.
  • Incredibly Low Tuition: Public university fees are calculated based on your family income (ISEE-Parificato). Fees typically range from €156 to €4,000 per year, and many international students qualify for regional scholarships that provide free tuition, free housing, and a stipend.
  • International Mobility: Because the program is in English, students are well-prepared to clear the USMLE (USA), PLAB/UKMLA (UK), or return to pass local exams like NEXT (India).

6 Alternative Options

If the IMAT doesn't pan out or the single-university choice restriction for non-EU students feels too risky, consider these strong alternatives:

1. Private Italian Medical Schools

Universities like Humanitas, San Raffaele, UniCamillus, and Cattolica offer excellent English MD programs.

The Catch: They do not use the IMAT; they host their own separate entrance exams (often online) earlier in the spring.

Cost: Tuition is higher, ranging from €10,000 to €23,000 per year.

2. Eastern European English Programs

Countries like Poland, Hungary, the Czech Republic, and Romania run highly established 6-year English medical programs.

The Catch: Entrance exams are university-specific and less standardized than the IMAT. Tuition ranges from €10,000 to €18,000 per year, with a slightly higher cost of living than subsidized Italian student life.

3. Cyprus and Malta

Both countries offer EU-standard medical degrees taught entirely in English. Malta features programs like Queen Mary University of London (Malta campus), combining a UK curriculum with an EU location.

The Catch: Tuition is premium, frequently matching or exceeding €20,000+ annually.

FAQs - MBBS in Italy

Is MBBS abroad a good option for Indian students?

Yes, MBBS abroad is highly recommended for students who could not get a government medical seat in India. Universities are NMC/WHO approved and degrees are globally recognized.

What is the total cost of MBBS abroad?

Total cost ranges from ₹ 18 Lacs (Uzbekistan) to ₹ 90 Lacs (Czech Republic). Popular options like Georgia cost ₹ 25-35 Lacs total including hostel and food.

Is NEET required for MBBS abroad?

Yes, NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students to be eligible for the NMC screening test (NExT/FMGE) after completing MBBS abroad.

Is there any donation or capitation fee?

No. In countries like Georgia, Uzbekistan, Russia, Kazakhstan and most European universities, there is NO donation. You pay only tuition and hostel fees.

How long does the admission process take?

Typically 30-60 days from application to visa. We handle the complete process including university application, documentation and visa.

Do you provide FMGE/NExT coaching?

Yes, we provide dedicated FMGE/NExT online coaching to our students so they can practice India alongside their MBBS abroad.
