At a Glance

Quick Facts About MBBS in Europe

English-medium programs at every country on this page (see note on Spain below).

6-year duration almost everywhere (5 years coursework + 1 year clinical internship).

NMC-compliant pathways available in Italy, Poland, Hungary, Romania, Czech Republic and Bulgaria.

No donation or capitation fees at any public university — tuition is the same for every applicant.

Tuition starting from approximately €500/year (Italy, income-based) up to €18,000/year (Hungary).

NEET qualification is mandatory for every Indian student, regardless of country.

Country-by-Country

Best Countries for MBBS in Europe

🇮🇹

Italy

The most selective and the most affordable, if you clear the entrance exam.

  • Public universities, tuition scaled to family income (as low as €500/year)
  • Entry through IMAT — a single national exam, roughly 135 seats for non-EU students across the country
  • 100% English-medium MBBS at every public university offering the international track
  • Best fit for strong students who can prepare seriously for one competitive exam
Explore MBBS in Italy
🇵🇱

Poland

The most established choice — large Indian student communities, no entrance exam.

  • English-medium programs at long-running medical universities (Warsaw, Gdansk, Poznan, and others)
  • Admission on 12th-grade merit — no separate entrance test
  • A well-worn path: more Indian alumni here than almost anywhere else in Europe
Explore MBBS in Poland
🇭🇺

Hungary

The most academically rigorous of the affordable options.

  • Long-established medical schools (Semmelweis, Pécs, Debrecen, Szeged) with strong global reputations
  • English-medium throughout, no entrance exam beyond NEET
  • A step up in cost from Poland or Romania, but with a correspondingly stronger academic reputation
Explore MBBS in Hungary
🇷🇴

Romania

The most budget-friendly EU degree with a genuine track record.

  • Tuition among the lowest in the EU for an English-medium MBBS
  • Admission by ranking list — no entrance exam
  • A trusted, long-standing destination, including at well-known institutions like Carol Davila University in Bucharest
Explore MBBS in Romania
🇧🇬

Bulgaria

Direct admission, lower living costs, same EU recognition.

  • No entrance exam beyond NEET — admission is straightforward and merit-based
  • One of the lowest total-cost options in this comparison
  • A smaller but well-established Indian student presence
Explore MBBS in Bulgaria
🇪🇸

Spain — read this before you consider it

Spain's public medical degree ("Grado en Medicina") is taught in Spanish, not English. We've flagged exactly why this matters below the comparison table — please read it before treating Spain as a like-for-like option with the five countries above.

MBBS in Europe vs. MBBS in Georgia

A common comparison for students weighing affordability against EU status.

Degree status

Georgia is a non-EU degree; all five European countries above carry full EU recognition.

Tuition

Broadly comparable to Romania/Bulgaria at the lower end.

Residency

An EU degree opens pathways to work or specialise anywhere in the EU after licensing; Georgia does not carry the same regional mobility.

Language

Georgia's English-medium programs are well-established and NMC-compliant, same as the European options here.

Bottom line

If long-term EU residency matters to you, Europe has the edge; if minimising upfront cost is the only priority, Georgia and Bulgaria are close competitors.

MBBS in Europe vs. MBBS in Russia

Cost

Russia is typically cheaper at the low end, but the range is wide — some Russian universities cost more than Romania or Bulgaria.

Clinical exposure

Both offer strong hands-on training; Russia's programs are generally larger and more established for international students.

Student lifestyle

Europe (EU) offers Schengen travel and a different cultural environment than Russia.

Degree recognition

Both are NMC-compliant when English-medium requirements are met — always verify per university.

Bottom line

This comparison usually comes down to budget and personal preference rather than one being objectively better.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MBBS in Europe recognised by NMC?

Yes, for the countries listed above (Italy, Poland, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria), provided the specific university and program meet NMC's Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) Regulations, 2021 — a minimum 54-month course taught entirely in English, plus a 12-month internship at the same institution. Spain is the exception; see the callout above.

Can Indian students study medicine in Europe in English?

Yes, at all five countries above. Spain is the one major exception — its public medical degree is taught in Spanish.

Which European country offers the lowest total cost?

Bulgaria and Romania are typically the most affordable, with Italy's public universities matching or beating them for students who clear IMAT and qualify for income-based fee reductions.

Is IMAT compulsory for all of Europe?

No — IMAT is specific to Italy. Poland, Hungary, Romania, and Bulgaria do not require a separate entrance exam beyond NEET.

Can students work part-time while studying?

EU student visas generally permit limited part-time work, though rules and hour limits vary by country. We'll walk you through the specifics for your chosen destination.

Which country is best for Indian students overall?

There's no single answer — it depends on your NEET score, budget, and whether you're willing to prepare for IMAT. Poland and Romania suit students who want a straightforward, no-entrance-exam path; Italy and Hungary suit students aiming for stronger academic reputations; Bulgaria suits the most budget-conscious applicants.

What is the total cost of MBBS in Europe?

Across the five recommended countries, total 6-year cost (tuition + living expenses) ranges from roughly ₹20 lakhs (Bulgaria) to ₹1.1 crore (Hungary, upper end), depending on the country and university. See the comparison table above for a country-by-country breakdown.
